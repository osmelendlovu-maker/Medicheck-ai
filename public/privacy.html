Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

MediCheck AI ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. What We Collect

Symptom descriptions you enter. When you use the symptom checker, the text you type is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate general health guidance. We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or any other identifying information to use the free tier.

Usage data stored on your device. Your daily free-check count and check history are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers or stored in any database we control.

Payment information, if you subscribe. If you upgrade to MediCheck AI Plus, your payment is processed entirely by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. We do not see or store your card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Symptom text is sent to our AI provider solely to generate the guidance shown to you in that session. We do not use it to build a profile of you, sell it, or share it for advertising. We do not currently store your symptom history on any server — it exists only in your browser until you clear your browser data.

3. Third Parties We Use

Groq (AI processing) — receives the symptom text you submit to generate a response.
Lemon Squeezy (payments) — handles subscription billing if you upgrade.
Vercel (hosting) — hosts this website. Standard web server logs may be collected automatically.

4. Your Rights (POPIA & General)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, request correction or deletion, object to processing, and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa). Since we don't store an account tied to you, most of your data can be cleared by clearing your browser's site data for this app.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information in transit (e.g., using HTTPS) but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

MediCheck AI is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of MediCheck AI after changes means you accept the revised policy.
